### Item Stand
A simple stand that holds and displays any one item, much like the item frame!

Insert item by right-clicking, and remove it by right-clicking again
with an empty hand.

Recipe unlocks with an iron ingot.

### Candle Tray

Holds either a candle or torch and acts as a light source equivalent to the held item.
Useful for decorating fences, posts or walls!

It is compatible with modded candles, such as from Mortar. Candles in the tray cannot be extinguished.
Redstone torches in the tray do not output redstone signal (may be changed).

Recipe unlocks with an iron ingot.

### Bamboo Flower Pot
A bamboo flower pot! Functionally similar to the vanilla flower pot,
however Decorum makes a couple of changes:
- The flower pot changes shape depending on what is planted in it! Saplings get wider pots than flowers.
- The flower pot renders the entire plant model instead of just the texture, which means decent compatibility with custom flower models, including texture variants. 

Because of this the bamboo flower pot should also work with modded flowers!
(Though right now some plants such as fern or bamboo cannot be used,
this will be implemented later!)

Recipe unlocks with a bamboo stick.

Inspiration taken from the resource pack [Fresh Flower Pots](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/fresh-flower-pots) by BlueHDGaming.
